================================================================================
                      JEEVA PET CARE - ADMIN & USER GUIDE
================================================================================

This guide explains how the JEEVA application works, focusing on what the Admin 
dashboard does, how Pet Owners and Vets use the app, and the rules in place.


================================================================================
1. ADMIN OVERVIEW
================================================================================

* Signing In to Admin
  - Admin accounts are designated securely on the backend (not through public signup).
  - Open the JEEVA app and log in with your Admin email and password.
  - The app automatically recognizes your Admin role and opens the Admin Dashboard.

* What the Dashboard Numbers Mean
  - Pet Owners: Total number of registered pet owner accounts.
  - Verified Vets: Total number of approved veterinary doctors.
  - Administrators: Total number of active platform administrators.
  - Registered Pets: Total number of pets created by pet owners.
  - Pending Vet Reviews: Applications waiting for your approval or rejection.
  - Active Announcements: In-app notices currently visible to users.
  - Published Guides: Articles currently live in the Explore care library.
  - Recorded Visits: Total veterinary visit records logged by owners.
  All numbers come directly from live database records (no estimates or fake stats).


================================================================================
2. ADMIN FEATURES
================================================================================

* User Search & Management
  - What it does: Lets you find any registered user, view their basic details, and check their status.
  - Where to find it: Tap the "Users" tab in the Admin navigation bar.
  - How to use it: Type a name or email into the search bar, or filter by role (Pet Owner, Vet, Admin) and status (Active, Suspended).
  - What happens: The list instantly updates with matching user profiles.

* Account Suspension & Reactivation
  - What it does: Temporarily locks a user out of the platform for rule violations.
  - Where to find it: In the "Users" tab, tap any user to open their details card.
  - How to use it: Tap "Suspend Account", enter a clear reason, and confirm. To restore access, tap "Reactivate Account".
  - What happens: When suspended, all active login sessions for that user are deleted immediately.
  - What the user sees: If they try to log in or use the app, they receive an alert stating their account is suspended with the reason provided.

* Vet Applications, Approval & Rejection
  - What it does: Reviews self-submitted credentials from pet owners who want to be recognized as Vets.
  - Where to find it: Tap the "Vet Applications" tab.
  - How to use it: Filter between Pending, Approved, or Rejected tabs. Tap an application to review qualifications, council registration number, clinic name, and address.
  - Approving: Tap "Approve Application". The user's role is immediately changed from Pet Owner to Vet, and they are recorded as verified.
  - Rejecting: Tap "Reject Application" and enter a specific reason (such as missing license number).
  - What the applicant sees: The applicant's profile immediately updates with the decision. If rejected, they can see your feedback, correct their details, and resubmit. Past reviews and resubmissions are kept in review history.

* Pet & Appointment Oversight
  - What it does: Gives high-level oversight of registered animals and owner-logged vet visits across the platform.
  - Where to find it: Tap the "Pets" or "Appointments" tabs.
  - How to use it: Search by pet name, breed, species, clinic name, or status.
  - What happens: You can monitor platform activity while respecting user privacy.

* Care Guide Content Management
  - What it does: Lets you write, publish, update, and organize care articles for pet owners.
  - Where to find it: Tap the "Guides" tab.
  - How to use it: Tap "+ New Guide" to write an article with a title, category, reading time, and content. Choose "Publish" to make it live or "Draft" to keep it private. You can toggle any guide between Published and Draft at any time.
  - What happens: Published guides appear immediately in the Explore tab for all pet owners.

* In-App Announcements
  - What it does: Broadcasts important news, updates, or maintenance alerts to users.
  - Where to find it: Tap the "Announcements" tab.
  - How to use it: Tap "+ New Announcement", enter a title and message, and choose the target audience (All Users, Pet Owners, or Vets).
  - What happens: Published announcements appear in the Notifications center for the targeted audience inside the app.

* Activity & Audit History
  - What it does: Provides a tamper-evident, read-only activity stream of all important administrative actions.
  - Where to find it: Tap the "Audit Logs" tab.
  - How to use it: Filter by action type (Suspensions, Reactivations, Vet Approvals, Content Changes).
  - What happens: Each entry displays who performed the action, the target user or item, the exact timestamp, and the reason given.

* Community Moderation Tab
  - What it does: Displays platform status and configuration for community interactions.
  - Where to find it: Tap the "Community" tab.
  - Note: Connects to forum post oversight when user-generated public posting is enabled on the backend.

* Logout
  - Tap the "Logout" button in the dashboard header to securely end your session and return to the login screen.

3. IMPORTANT ADMIN RULES & SAFETY CONTROLS
================================================================================

* Admin Access Control:
  - Only authenticated users with the Admin role can access Admin screens and backend endpoints.
  - Public signup can never create an Admin account.

* Pet Privacy Protection:
  - Admins can view basic pet info (name, species, breed, age) and general visit records.
  - Admins CANNOT view private clinical health records, medical diagnoses, or prescription notes. These remain strictly private between the pet owner and their vet.

* Protection Against Self-Lockout & Orphaned Systems:
  - An Admin cannot suspend their own account.
  - The system will block any attempt to suspend the last remaining active Admin.

* Scope of Vet Approval:
  - Approving a Vet application grants the Vet role badge and unlocks professional profile features.
  - Vet approval does NOT grant automatic access to other pet owners' private records without direct owner consent.

* Nature of Notifications:
  - Announcements and reminders are delivered inside the app (in-app alerts and notifications screen).
  - They are not external SMS, phone push notifications, or emails.

4. PET OWNER SIDE — SHORT SUMMARY
================================================================================

* Account & Profile:
  - Sign up with name, email, and password. Update name anytime in Profile.
* Pets:
  - Add, view, edit, and manage pet profiles (name, species, breed, gender, birth date, weight, microchip ID).
* Vaccinations:
  - Record administered vaccines, batch numbers, dates, and upcoming due dates.
* Appointments:
  - Log past and upcoming vet clinic visits, doctor names, purposes, and follow-up notes.
* Health Records:
  - Keep private logs of allergies, chronic conditions, surgeries, and weight history.
* Medications:
  - Track active medications, dosages, frequency, and treatment duration.
* Reminders & Guides:
  - View dynamic home reminders for vaccines and meds. Read curated care guides in Explore.
* Applying as a Vet:
  - A Pet Owner who is a licensed veterinarian can submit their professional credentials from their Profile screen.
* How Admin Decisions Affect Owners:
  - If an Admin suspends an account, the owner cannot log in until reactivated.
  - When an Admin approves their Vet application, their account upgrades to Vet status.
  - When an Admin posts announcements or guides, owners see them in their app feed.


================================================================================
5. VET SIDE — SHORT SUMMARY
================================================================================

* What Approved Vets Can Do:
  - View their verified Vet status badge and professional registration details.
  - Access their professional dashboard and receive targeted Vet announcements from Admin.
  - Maintain their own personal pets, health logs, and records.

* Unfinished Vet Features:
  - Direct live appointment scheduling between owners and clinics.
  - Direct vet-to-patient digital prescription issuance.
  These workflows are planned for upcoming releases.
